# 📖 JournalTOCs Redesign

> "Explore the World of Research"

A UI/UX redesign of [JournalTOCs](http://www.journaltocs.hw.ac.uk) — the free
scholarly journal Tables of Contents (TOC) discovery platform, reimagining its
navigation, colour system, layout, and responsiveness for a cleaner, more
usable research experience. The very first project, built as part of F27WD Web Design and Databases coursework
at Heriot-Watt University Dubai (Group 14).

---

## 📌 About the Project

JournalTOCs is the largest free collection of scholarly journal TOCs —
26,148 journals from 3,359 publishers but its original interface was
outdated, with a cluttered navigation bar, poor colour contrast, and no
mobile responsiveness. This project redesigns the platform into a modern,
user-friendly experience while keeping its core purpose intact: helping
researchers, students, and librarians search, browse, and follow journals
for the latest scholarly publications.

The goal was to improve the site's design, navigation, and functionality
end-to-end, from the homepage to the sign up flow, subject and publisher
directories, and the about/contact page, through a full UI/UX and CSS
overhaul.

---

## ✨ Key Improvements

- **Navigation:** Replaced the confusing dual search/browse bar with a
  single, structured nav bar with a clear, prominent search field
- **Sign In / Sign Up:** Moved authentication out of the page body and into
  an accessible nav bar icon with a modal-based login/signup flow
- **Colour system:** Replaced the low-contrast dark grey background and
  clashing orange/white text with a unified warm, accessible colour palette
  across the whole site
- **Layout:** Reorganised page content so instructions sit directly above
  their related controls (e.g. "follow a journal" instructions above the
  checkbox list) instead of being buried below
- **Subjects & Publishers A–Z:** Restructured into a searchable,
  alphabetically consistent directory for faster navigation
- **About / Contact:** Separated social links and contact details into a
  dedicated Contact Us section, moving user testimonials under a proposed
  Feedback subsection
- **Performance & responsiveness:** Added smooth page transition animations
  and mobile-friendly responsive layouts (nav bar, hero text, and content
  reflow on smaller screens)
